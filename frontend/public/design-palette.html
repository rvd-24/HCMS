Open questions for design

Things the code does today that we'd like a decision on.

  1. Primary button contrast. White 14px text on the brand-400→500 gradient is 2.4–3.2:1. Darken the fill (brand-600 is 4.6:1) or keep the fresh green and accept it for large buttons only?
  2. ink-faint is used for real text — table headers, placeholders, timestamps — at 3.1:1 on white and 2.8:1 on canvas. Darken the token, or limit it to decoration?
  3. Two greens for status. "Confirmed" uses Tailwind emerald while "Completed" uses brand green; they're close enough to confuse. Should one move to another hue?
  4. Small coral and gray badges (Cancelled, Off, No-show) sit just under 4.5:1 at 11px.
  5. Colours outside the theme. Chart series, tooth findings, avatars and the status tones use hex or Tailwind defaults rather than theme tokens. Bring them into the palette?
  6. Decay orange (#E0901B) is 2.6:1 on white — below the 3:1 for chart marks — and sits close to the "missed" chart orange.
  7. Dark mode. Not built. Wanted for clinic night shifts, or out of scope?
